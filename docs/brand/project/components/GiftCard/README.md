The template card in the gallery: a 3:4 preview of the gift with its name, one-line description and price.

**Provide** an occasion palette (ground, text, accent), a motif, a kicker (2–3 words), a title (one sentence in `card-serif`) and the price in ₹. Source: `TemplateCard` and `TemplatePreview` in `src/components/templates/`.

- Corners `radius-card`, elevation `shadow-card`; the card tilts toward the pointer on desktop.
- The title is the gift's voice, not a product description: "Another lap around the sun, together."
- One motif per card, from the `Motifs` set or the card's own SVG; never photos on the gallery card.
