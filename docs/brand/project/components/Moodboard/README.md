The TyniTales moodboard: the feeling every screen, gift and campaign should land on.

- **Handwritten, not printed.** Paper, ink and a real signature beat glossy cards.
- **Candlelit, not confetti cannon.** Warm, low light; celebration saved for the payoff moment.
- **Small on purpose.** One person, one page, one feeling. Generous space, few elements.
- **Sealed with love.** The wax seal (rose full stop) is the brand's signature gesture.
- **Kept forever.** Polaroids on a fridge, a note in a coat pocket: things people keep.

Real moments to draw from: chai at 2am, Diwali lights on a balcony, a Goa trip finally taken, monsoon walks, birthday candles at midnight, a voice note from Mom.
