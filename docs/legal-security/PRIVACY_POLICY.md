**Who we are.** TyniTales (tynitales.com) is run by **Tushar Bahadur**, Ranchi, Jharkhand, India ("we"). **[OWNER: add the full postal address, and the legal entity if you register one.]** Contact and grievance officer: Tushar Bahadur, Founder, support.tynitales@gmail.com.

## What we collect and how
- **Account:** your email; with Google sign-in also your name and picture (from Google); your sign-off if you set one.
- **Your gifts:** names, messages, photos, songs, stickers, schedule, optional password (stored only as a one-way hash), and open counts.
- **Drafts:** gifts in progress, saved in your browser and, when you're signed in, in your account (photos included).
- **Payments:** Razorpay processes them. You enter card, UPI or bank details in Razorpay's own window; they never reach us. We keep Razorpay's order and payment references, the amount and the date.
- **Messages:** your email and message when you contact us.
- **Technical:** IP address and browser info in hosting logs; a scrambled (keyed-hash) IP for one day to stop abuse; anonymous page-speed data (Vercel Speed Insights, no cookies).

We don't collect location, phone numbers, IDs, or use analytics or advertising trackers. There's no automated decision-making or profiling.

## Why
To make, store, show and deliver your gifts; run your account; take payments and refunds; answer you; and keep the service secure. Legal basis (DPDP Act 2023): your consent when you sign up and use the service, and legitimate uses such as meeting legal obligations (tax records). **[OWNER: confirm with a lawyer]**

## Who sees it
Your gifts can be opened by anyone with the link (and password, if set). Gift pages aren't indexed by search engines or listed publicly. We don't sell or rent personal data.

## Service providers
Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database, sign-in, storage), Razorpay (payments), Resend (email), Google (Google sign-in; our support inbox), YouTube (song playback). Some store data outside India, including the US. Details: `THIRD_PARTY_DATA_PROCESSORS.md`.

## How long
Account and gifts: until you delete them. Drafts: 60 days without changes. Abuse-prevention records: 1 day. Payment records: kept, unlinked from you, as tax law requires after account deletion **[OWNER: period]**. Messages: as long as needed **[OWNER: period]**. Provider backups expire on their own schedules. Details: `DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md`.

## Security
Row-level access rules, encryption in transit (HTTPS/HSTS), hashed gift passwords, server-only secrets, rate limits. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

## Cookies and storage
Only what's needed: sign-in session, drafts, a gift-unlock cookie, a few "seen it" flags. No ad or analytics cookies. Google, Razorpay and YouTube set their own only when you use them. Details: `COOKIE_POLICY.md`.

## Your rights
- **See:** profile → Your data.
- **Download:** profile → Download my data.
- **Correct:** name and sign-off on your profile; gifts while editable; email by writing to us.
- **Delete:** profile → Delete account.
- **Withdraw consent:** stop using the service and delete your account.
- **Complain:** to our grievance officer (acknowledged within 48 hours, resolved within a month), or to the Data Protection Board of India.
