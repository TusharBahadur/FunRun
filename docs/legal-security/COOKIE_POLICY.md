# Cookie and storage policy: TyniTales

_Last updated: 17 November 2026._

TyniTales uses **no advertising or analytics cookies** and no cross-site tracking. Everything we store in your browser is needed for a feature you're using, so there's no consent banner: there's nothing optional to switch off. If we ever add optional tracking, we'll ask first and add a way to change your mind.

## Ours

| Name | Type | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tt_gift_<link>` | Cookie (HttpOnly, secure, only sent to that gift's page) | Remembers you unlocked a password-protected gift on this device | 1 year |
| `sb-…-auth-token` | localStorage | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out or the session expires |
| `tynitales` database | IndexedDB | Drafts of gifts you're making | Until sent, deleted, or 60 days untouched |
| `tynitales:signin-nudge`, tour flag, `tynitales:opened:<link>` | localStorage | "Seen it already" flags; not counting your own views of gifts you sent | Until cleared |
| `tynitales:publish:<id>`, `tynitales:continue-chip-hidden` | sessionStorage | Remembering checkout choices across sign-in; hiding the "continue" chip | Until the tab closes |

## Others' (only when you use the feature)
- **Google** sets its own cookies on Google's domain when you choose "Continue with Google".
- **Razorpay**'s payment window sets its own cookies (fraud prevention) when you pay.
- **YouTube** (privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com`) loads only when a song is played.
- **Vercel Speed Insights** measures page speed **without cookies or identifiers**.

You can clear all of this in your browser settings; signing out clears the session.
